Ember box - quick start
=======================

What you need
-------------
* This SD card (already prepared - you're reading it!)
* An Orange Pi Zero 4 and a USB-C power supply (5V / 3A or better)
* Your Creality Falcon2 laser, and a USB webcam if you want the camera view
  (plug both into a powered USB hub, then the hub into the board's USB-C data port)
* Your home network (a cable to the router, or Wi-Fi)

Step 1 - Wi-Fi  (skip if you plug the board into your router with a cable)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Open  ember-config.txt  on this drive (double-click it, it opens in Notepad).
Fill in:
    wifi_name=YourWiFiName
    wifi_password=YourWiFiPassword
Save the file. Eject the SD card safely.

Step 2 - Start the box
----------------------
Put the SD card into the board and plug in the power.
The first start takes about 2 minutes (it sets itself up). Later starts are faster.

Step 3 - Open Ember
-------------------
On a computer, phone or tablet on the same network, open  http://ember.local
The first time, Ember asks you to create the owner account (a name and a
password). After that, everyone opens  http://ember.local  and signs in.
The owner adds other people from the Admin page and can give them roles.

No Wi-Fi set up and no network cable? After about 2 minutes the box starts
its own Wi-Fi called "Ember-Setup-...." (password: ember1234). Join it and
open  http://10.42.0.1

The file MY-ACCESS-LINK.txt on this card (written after the first start) lists
the box's addresses and the OWNER LINK, which resets the owner password if
it is ever lost. Keep that link private.

Then: Device tab > pick the laser's USB port > Connect.

Doesn't open?
 * Wait one more minute and try again.
 * Look in your router's app for a device called "ember" and open its address,
   e.g. http://192.168.1.50

Updates
-------
When a new version is released, Ember shows "Update available" at the top.
Press Install. It never updates while a job is running.
